Jump into chaotic, cartoon-style missile battles where the goal is simple: hover, lock, and launch! Aim by hovering your cursor over other players to lock on, then fire your missile and watch the chaos.

⚠️ EARLY ACCESS: This game is still in active development. Expect updates, changes, and new features as we expand the experience. Feedback is welcome!

[Hover over a player] → Lock aim
[Click] → Fire missile
[Q] → Dash
[Double Space] → Double jump
[Jump into wall + spam W] → Wall climb
[G] → Emote panel

This is an early phase release, core systems are in place, but content and polish are still being added.
