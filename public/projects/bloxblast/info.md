Welcome to Blox Blast THE most relaxing puzzle game on roblox!
We welcome all feedback and value every suggestion, as it helps us continuously improve our experience!

- Game is in beta expect some bugs and a lot of updates!

🎮 How to Play
- Drag blocks onto the board.
- Clear rows/columns to crush cubes and score.
- Chain combos for massive rewards.
(Don’t let the board fill up!)

- Shop and skins
