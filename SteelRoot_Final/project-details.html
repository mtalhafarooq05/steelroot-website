About The Game

Radish Rumble is our flagship title where you play as angry root vegetables battling it out in the dirt! We designed this game with chaotic, fast-paced fun in mind.

Featuring over 50 unique maps ranging from tranquil gardens to hazardous radioactive greenhouses, players must use quick reflexes and strategy to survive.

Unlock hundreds of customizable skins, weapons, and taunts, and climb the ranks in our fully integrated competitive ranked mode.
